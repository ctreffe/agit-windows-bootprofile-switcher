# Project Idea Backlog

This project-owned backlog retains possibilities separately from the roadmap's
selected planned work and Decision Records' durable accepted choices. Capturing
an idea sets no priority, date or implementation commitment. An idea may be
selected immediately or revisited later.

Use `record-idea` when asked to retain an existing idea. Use explicit
`perform-retrospective` to develop improvements together from a project period.
Keep one authoritative entry with stable repository-local IDs. Cross-repository
writes require exact destination/action control-word authorization; otherwise
return a `Pending Idea Transfer`. Access and publication remain separate.

Preserve engineering evidence, architecture, test and release authority;
keep secrets, private logs and source dumps out of entries.

## Status Model

- **Captured** — retained without an implementation decision.
- **Exploring** — alternatives, evidence or constraints are being assessed.
- **Promoted** — selected into a linked roadmap or decision process.
- **Transferred** — the identified destination owns the idea; retain a redirect.
- **Deferred** — retained but deliberately inactive.
- **Rejected** — closed with a short rationale.

## Entry Format

```markdown
### IDEA-NNNN: Short title

- **Status:** Captured | Exploring | Promoted | Transferred | Deferred | Rejected
- **Opportunity:** Problem or useful outcome
- **Potential destination:** This project, verified template, Governance or unresolved
- **Next decision:** Smallest maintainer-owned decision
```

Add evidence, sources, risks, boundaries and references when material. A supplied
idea needs no reviewed period or recurrence. Retrospective-derived candidates
retain their reviewed period and sanitized evidence. Never renumber existing
IDs or copy the source template's ideas into this project.
